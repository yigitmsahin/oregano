Who we are

Three generations of one crop

Our family has farmed oregano on the limestone slopes above the Aegean since 1998. Today we work with 180 contracted growers across the region and run our own drying, cleaning and packing facility.

That means one thing for buyers: the oregano in your container was picked, dried and tested by the same people who sold it to you. No brokers, no blending of unknown lots, no surprises at customs.

  • Own farms and contracted growers under one agronomy programme
  • In-house lab: moisture, ash, pesticide and microbiology screening
  • Steam sterilisation without irradiation or ETO
  • Export documentation handled end to end

How it works

From field to container

Seven controlled steps, one traceable lot number.

  1. 01

    Harvest

    Cut at peak carvacrol, between June and August.

  2. 02

    Drying

    Shade-dried in controlled airflow to hold colour and oil.

  3. 03

    Cleaning

    De-stemming, sieving, gravity separation and metal detection.

  4. 04

    Sterilisation

    Saturated steam only — no irradiation, no ethylene oxide.

  5. 05

    Laboratory

    Moisture, ash, oil, microbiology and residue screening per lot.

  6. 06

    Packing

    Food-grade liners, sealed and palletised under your label.

  7. 07

    Shipping

    Loaded at Izmir with full documentation and lot traceability.
