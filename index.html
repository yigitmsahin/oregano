Who we are

Our roots are in oregano

For 45 years, our roots have been in oregano. From the fields of the Denizli region — one of Türkiye's finest growing areas for aromatic herbs — we have grown into one of the region's most established herb traders.

We sell you 45 years of knowing this crop: which fields yield the best quality, how to process it, and how to deliver it clean, consistent, and on time. What we bring is experience, reliability, and a commitment we stand behind on every shipment.

  • 45 years in oregano, from the Denizli region
  • Up to 3,000 MT of raw material traded every season
  • PA risk assessed field by field, before harvest
  • Clean, consistent, on-time delivery

Now we're taking that experience to the world. Our goal is simple: to build long-term partnerships, grow into a stronger operation, and offer better opportunities to the growers we work with and the buyers we serve.

If you're looking for a supplier who treats your order like their own name is on it — that's us.

PA compliance

PA risk, managed at the source

Every season we trade up to 3,000 MT of raw material. That scale, combined with our field experience, lets us manage pyrrolizidine alkaloid (PA) compliance risk at the source.

  1. 01

    Field assessment

    Before harvest, we observe each field and assess its PA risk.

  2. 02

    Separate processing

    Goods from low-risk fields are processed separately.

  3. 03

    Testing

    These goods are tested again after processing.
